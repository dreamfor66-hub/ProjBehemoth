# Direction and actual references

User supplied Darkest Dungeon Houndmaster (cutout + attack scene), fountain, pedestal proportion, floating dog and ordinary-frame boss rejection screenshots were opened and compared against actual B1-Walls, project original hooded archer and installed dog/mage.

Primary first-person art-direction interview: [Chris Bourassa, Red Hook, 2015-06-09](https://80.lv/articles/red-hook-studios-talks-about-the-creation-of-darkest-dungeon), especially discussion of pooled black shapes, empty eyes and weight. The interview does not prescribe copying assets or increasing noise. Our interpretation for this revision: mature muzzle and proportions, shadowed restrained eyes, unsmiling vigilance, equipment tied to an occupation, weight-bearing contact and black interior masses. Darkness alone is not acceptance evidence.

Hound: ash-grey adult corpse-recovery tracking hound; oxblood work collar, broken lead, brass recovery tag, scar and wrapped right foreleg. Rescue/world, companion idle/attack and relic must agree. Reject prior puppy face and airborne exploration position. Generated fur is examined at actual game size; no extra texture is added.

Fountain: remove angel completely; shallow broad basin, short central pedestal and wide plinth. Front/side/rear and drained states; poisonous and ordinary fonts intentionally indistinguishable before use (existing gamble preserved).

Entrances: complete heavy jamb/lintel/leaf paintings projected onto actual wall planes, covering the ordinary aperture/frame. Elite = square military iron lintel, heavy side posts; boss = monumental broad pentagonal lintel and deeply recessed paired leaves. No reliance on just a red leaf behind an unchanged arch.

Mage: replace burning-paper incompetence with return-route surveying. Map board and compass have actual supported grips and readable occupation. Existing white hair/pointed beard/indigo mantle identity remains. No unrelated hero repaints.

Mirror: retire the literal standing-mirror encounter from the random pool. A spring-trap alternative was produced and rejected on review: observing its rhythm, taking the spring and taking nearby coins were artificially exclusive physical actions. Both prototypes remain archived and excluded. The 19 remaining encounters fill the unchanged base event-room budget. See EventReview.md for every active premise and aftermath.

Actual runtime review found a left-side combat HUD occlusion on the hound and incomplete ground attachment on side facades. The hound now defaults to the right foreground with independently painted inward-facing idle/attack poses, changing to the left when actual nearby prop footprints occupy its support. It is drawn behind the hero on a stable floor anchor. The facade uses the shared vanishing point, physical floor support and a subdivided projective surface; an active complete facade suppresses the ordinary arch layer. Acceptance uses current Final-* runtime screenshots, not prior-task approvals. Final outcomes are recorded in Verification.md.
