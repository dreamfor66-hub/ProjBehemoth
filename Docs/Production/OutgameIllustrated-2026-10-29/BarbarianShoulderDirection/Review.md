# User reference correction — shoulder carry and axe design

Latest user feedback supersedes the earlier BarbarianGripRevision. That correction improved finger count but failed to check the whole tool direction; its installed result is rejected.

## Direct reference comparison

All three supplied pictures were opened before diagnosis/generation and preserved as User-Pose-Reference-*. The third reference matches the target's side: raised hand on viewer right holds the forward handle-end, axe head behind/above the supporting shoulder toward viewer left. References 1/2 show the equivalent relationship on the mirrored side. The user pictures are pose evidence only; stock character identities, costumes and watermarks are not adopted.

The former target put the heavy head next to the fist and a long butt out the opposite side. Corrected head is behind hood/shoulder, hand is on handle-end side and only a short butt extends past it. Four curled fingers and opposing thumb are read together with wrist, handle and support. Approved body appeal, low camera, jaw/expression, pelt and sunset composition were retained.

## Axe-head review and corrections

First direction-corrected generation (exec-0246...) replaced the established axe with a generic wide leaf/crescent plate. User correctly rejected that head. The original Barbarian-Front weapon crop was then opened directly. Its opposing flared wings, angular tips, pronounced throat cutouts, central eye/collar govern the replacement.

Second head version (exec-aaf...) copied too many small metal facets and did not line up the collar and exposed shaft; not installed. Third (exec-fa7...) simplified metal planes and made the collar legible. Its exposed handle angle was then locally straightened (exec-893...) so it reads as one rigid tool rather than two different directions concealed by fur. Review-Board.jpg shows original rejected direction versus installed result, plus enlarged head/socket and hand. Compare broad metal planes to original style authorities 03-armored-hood-fighter.jpg and 04-brawler-back.jpg, already opened for this work; no new skin/cloth detail is needed.
