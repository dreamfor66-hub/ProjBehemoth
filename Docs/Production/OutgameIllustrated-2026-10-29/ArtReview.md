# Narrative revision review

User feedback: over-conscious of original character-sheet poses; Monk and Barbarian especially awkward. Originals remain identity/costume authority, not a pose or camera constraint.

- Monk revised: removed the front-facing horse stance / enlarged spread thighs. He now feels a stone wall while walking a broken cloister walkway. One leading foot bears weight, rear foot and pelvis follow the walkway perspective; touch gives the open hand a physical purpose.
- Barbarian revised: removed floating broad squat, raised axe and isolated display of chest. Both hands now drive one straight haft into a split gate beam. Braced legs, rotated/leaning torso and contact point tell the same action. Existing human bear-pelt identity / leather trousers retained.
- Warrior: crouched shield-led exploration has a readable destination and shares the floor plane; sword stays low and aligned with hilt.
- Rogue: low movement around a pillar and daggers have coherent opposing arm paths; original sly expression remains recognizable.
- Wizard: book light and owl interaction provide a scene-specific action; staff grip and book support are legible.
- Samurai: drawing from a sheath uses aligned hilt/blade/socket and opposing hands; three-plate guard and male original identity retained.

All use black contour blocks and muted planes matching the playable hall. Generated illustrations deliberately keep the left information column dark and reserve lower contrast at the bottom. No original character-sheet files were edited. Final feel/readability is checked in the actual UI, not just standalone pictures.
