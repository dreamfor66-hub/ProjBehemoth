# MP / elemental build discussion — proposals only

No MP perk frequency, perk values, elemental relics or unlock rules were changed in this revision.

Keep MP and elemental perks available before owning a spell, per the user's earlier direction. Reduce their combined share of a common reward offer rather than gating them behind spell ownership. A candidate rule is at most one MP-related option among three, with the MP group carrying a low base offer weight. Wizard/class rewards and magical events can carry a higher weight. Exact weights need play feedback; none applied now.

Elemental conversion relics give weapon-focused classes a durable reason to invest in elemental damage. Recommended structure: accessible enchantment spells supply an early entry; rare conversion/proc relics expand the eventual ceiling. Rare relics alone cannot solve dead early elemental perks, since the needed relic may never appear.

- Fire normal-attack conversion + chance to burn: strong identity and clear fire-scaling hook.
- Lightning charge conversion: good second route. Unconditional +50% charge damage is likely to make it a generic best pickup even without lightning investment; separate/tune that payoff after the conversion rule exists.
- 30% attack-triggered lightning: approachable bridge. Specify attack versus successful hit and how multi-hit attacks roll; once per weapon action with a proc check on successful contact is a useful starting candidate, not implemented here.

Conversion should replace the damage element, preserving the attack's normal/charge classification and the existing weapon/charge/critical/stun modifiers. Then apply the converted element's multiplier once. It should not secretly become additional spell damage or gain an extra duplicate hit. Enchantments and conversion need an explicit overlap rule before implementation; avoid forcing an irreversible precedence from names/IDs.
